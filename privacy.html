Zemen Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · አማርኛ

Zemen is an Ethiopian calendar app for Android published by Biniam Kassahun (Gluon Technologies). This policy explains what Zemen does with your information. In short: Zemen collects nothing, and nothing leaves your phone.

Information Zemen uses

If you allow it, Zemen reads the calendars and events already on your phone, such as your Google Calendar, so it can show them on Ethiopian dates. It reads the calendar names, account names and colors, and each event's title, time, location and description. This is done on your phone each time the app shows them; Zemen does not copy your events anywhere else.

Zemen also saves your app settings on your device: your language, theme, which holidays and calendars to show, number style and the day your week starts. Calendar dates and holidays are calculated on the phone itself.

What we collect and share

Nothing. Zemen has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. Your calendar events and settings are never sent to us or to any third party. We cannot see your data.

Permissions

Zemen asks for the Calendar permission, and only to show your phone's events inside the app. Android describes this permission as reading and editing your calendar; this version of Zemen only reads it and does not change your events. You can deny the permission or turn it off at any time in your phone's settings, and Zemen keeps working as an Ethiopian calendar without your events. Zemen does not access your contacts, location or photos.

Deleting your data

Zemen does not store copies of your events, so there is nothing to delete beyond your settings. To remove them, clear Zemen's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

Zemen is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of Zemen handles your data differently, for example by letting you add or edit events, this policy will be updated on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: support@zemen.net.et
