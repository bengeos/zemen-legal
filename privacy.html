Zemen Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · አማርኛ

Zemen is an Ethiopian calendar app for Android published by Biniam Kassahun (Gluon Technologies). This policy explains what Zemen does with your information. In short: Zemen collects nothing, and nothing leaves your phone.

Information Zemen uses

If you allow it, Zemen reads the calendars and events already on your phone, such as your Google Calendar, so it can show them on Ethiopian dates. It reads the calendar names, account names and colors, and each event's title, time, location and description. This is done on your phone each time the app shows them; Zemen does not copy your events anywhere else.

Zemen also adds, changes and deletes events, but only the ones you ask it to, and it saves them in the calendar you choose on your phone. Events you create with an Ethiopian yearly or monthly repeat are written as separate events. Each one carries a small Zemen tag in the calendar, so the series can be edited together.

Zemen keeps a list of these series (title, dates and calendar) and your app settings on your device: your language, theme, which holidays and calendars to show, number style, the day your week starts and your reminder choices. Calendar dates and holidays are calculated on the phone itself.

Reminders

If you turn them on, Zemen shows notifications that it schedules on your phone, for holidays, fasts, saint days and your Ethiopian-date events. These reminders are created and shown on the phone itself; nothing is sent to a server.

What we collect and share

Nothing. Zemen has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. Your calendar events and settings are never sent to us or to any third party. We cannot see your data. Zemen also turns off Android's app backup, so its data is not copied to cloud backups. If you put an event in a synced calendar, such as Google Calendar, that calendar's own service syncs it, as with any calendar app.

Permissions

Zemen asks for these permissions:

  • Calendar (read and edit): to show your events and to save the events you add, change or delete.
  • Notifications: to show Zemen reminders.
  • Run at startup: to put your scheduled reminders back after your phone restarts.

You can deny any of these permissions or turn them off at any time in your phone's settings, and Zemen keeps working as an Ethiopian calendar. Zemen does not access your contacts, location or photos.

Deleting your data

Events Zemen created stay in your calendar until you delete them, in Zemen or in any calendar app. Uninstalling Zemen, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, removes its settings, its list of series and its scheduled reminders.

Children

Zemen is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

This policy may be updated on this page with a new effective date.

ዘመን የእነዚህን ተከታታይ ቀጠሮዎች ዝርዝር (ርዕስ፣ ቀኖችና የቀን መቁጠሪያ) እንዲሁም የመተግበሪያውን ቅንብሮች በስልክዎ ላይ ያስቀምጣል፦ ቋንቋዎን፣ ገጽታውን፣ የትኞቹ በዓላትና የቀን መቁጠሪያዎች እንዲታዩ፣ የቁጥር አጻጻፍን፣ ሳምንቱ የሚጀምርበትን ቀን እና የማስታወሻዎች ምርጫዎን። ቀኖችና በዓላት የሚሰሉት በስልኩ ላይ ነው።
