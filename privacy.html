Zemen Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · አማርኛ

Zemen is an Ethiopian calendar app for Android published by Biniam Kassahun (Gluon Technologies). This policy explains what Zemen does with your information. In short: Zemen collects nothing, and nothing leaves your phone.

Information Zemen stores

Zemen saves only your app settings on your device: your language, theme, which holidays to show, number style and the day your week starts. Calendar dates and holidays are calculated on the phone itself.

What we collect and share

Nothing. Zemen has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet. Your settings are never sent to us or to any third party. We cannot see your data.

Permissions

Zemen does not ask for any special permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, photos or your phone's calendar.

Deleting your data

To remove your settings, clear Zemen's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

Children

Zemen is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of Zemen handles your data differently, for example by syncing with your calendar, this policy will be updated on this page with a new effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions: support@zemen.net.et
