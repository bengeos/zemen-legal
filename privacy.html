Zemen Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026 · አማርኛ

Zemen is an Ethiopian calendar app for Android published by Biniam Kassahun (Gluon Technologies). This policy explains what Zemen does with your information. In short: nothing leaves your phone.

Information Zemen uses

With your permission, Zemen reads and writes the calendar on your device so it can show your events on Ethiopian dates and let you add, change or delete them. Zemen also stores your settings, such as your language choice, on your device.

What we collect and share

Nothing. Zemen has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your calendar events and settings are processed only on your device and are never sent to us or to any third party. We cannot see your data.

Permissions

Zemen asks for the Calendar permission only to show and edit your events inside the app. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings; the calendar views keep working without your events.

Deleting your data

Events you create in Zemen are stored in your phone's calendar like any other event, and you can delete them from Zemen or any calendar app. Uninstalling Zemen removes its settings from your device.

Children

Zemen is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions: support@zemen.net.et
